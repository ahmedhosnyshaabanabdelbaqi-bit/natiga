## Rules
- Ticket numbers unique; every status change recorded (history) and never deletable; closed tickets keep full history (model guard prevents deleting messages/history). Reopen by member within 14 days moves to open with reopen_count++.
- SLA: due dates computed from the policy at creation/priority change; overdue tickets listed on the admin dashboard and raised as OperationsExceptions (support category) by a scheduled job; first response = first non-internal staff/partner message.
- Escalation: manual (staff) or automatic when resolution overdue by > 24h (setting); `escalated` notifies operations managers (Notify).
- Visibility: member sees own tickets + non-internal messages; partner sees tickets related to their center (service_center_id) and only non-internal messages; staff by permission. Complaints against a center are copied to the partner's queue (waiting_partner) and the partner can reply.
- Related entity picker in the member form lists the member's own orders/bookings/deliveries/payments/events (each guarded by `Schema::hasTable`).
- Reviews: creation allowed only when `ReviewEligibility::for($user, $reviewable)` finds a verified completed booking (`maintenance_bookings.status = completed`) or a delivered order item — implement the checks against the contract tables with `Schema::hasTable` guards; moderation (approve/reject/hide with reason); partner reply; aggregate `ReviewSummary::for($reviewable)` (average, count, distribution) cached; public display only approved & verified; no anonymous unverified reviews.

## Pages
- Member: `/account/support` (tickets list with status/SLA badges), create (category, related entity, description, attachments via FileUpload + SafeUpload), detail (conversation, reply, close/reopen, rate satisfaction), `/account/reviews` (write reviews for eligible bookings/purchases, see status).
- Partner: `/partner/support` (tickets/complaints about the center, reply, request escalation), `/partner/reviews` (approved reviews, reply).
- Admin: `/admin/support` (DataTable: filters status/category/priority/assignee/overdue/center; bulk assign), ticket detail (timeline, internal notes, assign, priority, status transitions with reason, escalate, merge? no), SLA policies CRUD, dashboard cards (open by priority, overdue, first-response breaches), `/admin/reviews` (moderation queue, filters, decisions), exporters (tickets). KPIs `open_complaints` (tone warning), `tickets_overdue`; rate limiter `support-tickets` (5/hour per user); health check for overdue tickets.
